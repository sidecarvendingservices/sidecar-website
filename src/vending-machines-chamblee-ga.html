Can you put machines in a dealership service department and customer lounge?

Yes, and we recommend both. They're different audiences: technicians want strong caffeine and substantial food on short breaks, while customers waiting on a service appointment want water, coffee and snacks and will spend more. Chamblee's Peachtree Boulevard dealership corridor is dense enough that we can standardize the same two-machine package across multiple stores.

Do you carry international and Spanish-language products?

Yes. Chamblee sits inside DeKalb's designated international corridor, with a large share of residents speaking a language other than English at home and a meaningful share who speak English less than very well. We carry Latin American and Asian snack and beverage lines, label payment and refund instructions in multiple languages, and use icons so the machine is usable without reading either. Tell us what your people buy and we'll build the mix around it.

Do your machines take cash as well as cards?

Yes, and in Chamblee we treat that as a requirement rather than an option. Cashless-only machines exclude customers in a city with a large foreign-born population and a real share of cash-preferring households. We keep bill validators and coin mechanisms serviced and working, and we also accept cards and mobile wallets on every machine.

Do you serve the warehouse and flex buildings off Chamblee Tucker Road?

Yes — this is one of our strongest verticals here. Chamblee has modern tilt-wall warehouse and flex space inside I-285, which is unusually scarce this close to the perimeter, plus a multi-building technology park on DeKalb Technology Parkway. We stock those machines for early shifts: larger portions, hot coffee, sports and energy drinks, and bottled water in volume.
