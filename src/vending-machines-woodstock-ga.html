Common Questions

Woodstock vending, answered.

Do you cover Towne Lake as well as Woodstock?

Yes. Towne Lake sits in zip 30189 in unincorporated Cherokee County, and it's fully inside our service area, including the Towne Lake Parkway and Eagle Drive commercial district. We treat it as its own part of the route because it's physically separate from Downtown Woodstock and Ridgewalk.

Which Woodstock zip codes do you serve?

Three: 30188, which covers the city including Downtown Woodstock and the Ridgewalk corridor; 30189, which covers Towne Lake in unincorporated Cherokee County; and 30102, which is unincorporated Cobb County toward I-75 Exit 277 and includes the Cherokee Parkway and Northpoint Parkway business parks.

Is there enough of a daytime workforce here to justify a machine?

Yes, more than the headline numbers suggest. Woodstock's comprehensive plan reports roughly 17,300 workers commuting into the city each weekday against about 14,400 commuting out, in a city of around 39,400 residents. The net change looks small because similar numbers move in both directions, but the weekday population inside those buildings is real.

Do you place machines in apartment communities here?

Not as a focus. Woodstock caps new multifamily developments at under 100 units, which puts a hard ceiling on how much volume a single resident-facing machine can generate. We concentrate this market on offices, business parks, industrial sites and dealership service departments instead, where the headcount supports it.

Will construction on the I-575 interchange affect service?

It can affect timing, and we'd rather say so upfront. The Ridgewalk Parkway interchange at I-575 is being improved, and that's the main access point for the city's employment corridor. Our 34-minute figure is an off-peak estimate that doesn't account for construction delays, so we build restock schedules with slack in them.
