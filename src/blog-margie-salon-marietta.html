Now Serving Margie Salon In Marietta, GA

We're thrilled to be partnering with Margie Salon to bring a little something extra to clients waiting for their next service.

BM
Brian Co-founder, Sidecar Vending Services · August 9, 2026

We're excited to share our newest partnership: Sidecar is now the vending provider for Margie Salon in Marietta, Georgia. If you've been in for a color, a cut, or a treatment lately, you may have already noticed the new machine by the waiting area.

Waiting for hair services shouldn't mean going without

Color processing, treatments, and multi-step services can mean a real chunk of time in the chair or in the waiting area, and clients don't always plan ahead with a snack or a drink in hand. That's the gap we're filling at Margie Salon: a refrigerated smart vending machine stocked with a variety of drinks and snacks, so there's something for every taste while clients wait.

What's in the machine

We stocked the machine with a mix of cold drinks and grab-and-go snacks, from the familiar favorites to some lighter and better-for-you options, so there's something for everyone regardless of what they're in the mood for. And because it's one of our smart machines, we're able to track what's actually selling and adjust the mix over time rather than guessing at what belongs on the shelf.

A nice touch for clients, zero hassle for the salon

For Margie Salon, this is a free amenity — no cost to install, no inventory to manage, no restocking to think about. We handle sourcing, stocking, and keeping the machine running, and the salon gets to offer clients one more small reason to enjoy the wait.

Looking forward to what's next

We're looking forward to continuing our partnership with Margie Salon and making sure the machine stays stocked with what clients actually want. If your salon, spa, or waiting area could use the same amenity, we'd love to talk.
