Vending Machine Installation and Service in Marietta, Georgia

Marietta's resident population is about 63,000, but its daytime population swells by an estimated 85.5% as workers commute in — the largest daytime-population swing of any city in the Cobb/Cherokee area. Those people fill office parks, industrial buildings and the Wellstar Kennestone hospital campus, and most of those buildings have no food service on site. Sidecar Vending Services installs and maintains snack and beverage machines across Marietta and East Cobb at no cost to the property. We are based in Sandy Springs, 24 minutes away off-peak.

Medical & Healthcare

The Wellstar Kennestone Regional Medical Center campus on Church Street is a multi-tower acute-care facility, and its new nine-story, 235-bed Yellow Tower is scheduled to open April 15, 2026. Marietta zones the surrounding area as a Health Services district, and medical offices, dialysis centers and hospice facilities cluster on Kennestone Circle, Roswell Street, Windy Hill Road, Bells Ferry Road and Newmarket Parkway. We build these machines for 24-hour staffing: overnight caffeine, real food rather than only candy, and a nutrition-forward line for clinical staff.

📦

Warehouses & Industrial

Marietta has a dedicated Office/Warehouse district and a dedicated Industrial district in its comprehensive plan, plus named parks including Marble Mill Industrial, Franklin Forest Industrial, Cumberland Point Industrial, Allgood Industrial, Delk Industrial, the Marietta-Cobb Industrial Parks East and West, West Side Industrial Park and the Northgate Distribution Center. For shift operations we stock larger portions, energy and sports drinks, meat snacks and hot-beverage support, and set restock frequency so a machine isn't empty by second shift.

Offices & Coworking

The Northwest Atlanta office submarket that contains Marietta held roughly 29.2 million square feet at 18.7% vacancy and $29.45 per square foot in early 2026. In a market where landlords compete on amenity, a stocked machine is something a leasing agent can point to on a tour. Because Marietta's office stock is suburban park product occupied largely by smaller tenants, we usually recommend combination snack-and-drink units over separate towers.

Apartment communities and auto dealerships are secondary opportunities here. Marietta has designated four higher-density redevelopment corridors — Franklin Gateway, City Center South Renaissance, the South Marietta Parkway Activity Center and the Delk Road transit-oriented district — and seven franchise dealership brands are present in the city, mostly worked lot by lot rather than as one corridor.

Common Questions

Marietta vending, answered.

Do you serve East Cobb as well as Marietta proper?

Yes. East Cobb zip codes 30062, 30066 and 30068 are all within our Marietta service area, and we run East Cobb as its own service loop separate from west and south Marietta. That includes the Indian Hills, Paper Mill, East Cobb Park, Highland Pointe, Northampton, Windsor Oaks and Atlanta Country Club areas, along with the Sandy Plains corridor.

How long does it take you to reach Marietta from your base?

Our base is in Sandy Springs, 14.6 road miles from Marietta Square — about 24 minutes off-peak via I-285 West and I-75 North. That is a free-flow estimate, not a rush-hour time; the I-285 West to I-75 North movement is slow at peak, which is why we schedule Marietta service mid-morning or early afternoon rather than at 5pm.

Can you service a building that runs more than one shift?

Yes, and Marietta has a lot of them. The city zones dedicated Office/Warehouse and Industrial districts and contains named distribution and industrial parks, while the Kennestone hospital campus runs around the clock. For multi-shift sites we change the product mix — larger portions, energy and sports drinks, meat snacks, hot beverage support — and raise restock frequency so second and third shift aren't working an empty machine.

Which Marietta zip codes do you cover?

30008, 30060, 30062, 30064, 30066, 30067 and 30068. That spans the Marietta CBD and Square, Franklin Gateway, the Powers Ferry/Delk Road corridor, West Marietta and Kennesaw Mountain, northeast Cobb along Sandy Plains, and all of East Cobb.

Do you place machines in medical offices, not just hospitals?

Yes. Marietta's medical uses extend well past the main hospital campus into freestanding buildings on Kennestone Circle, Roswell Street, Cobb Parkway North, Windy Hill Road, Powers Ferry Road, Newmarket Parkway, Village Parkway and Bells Ferry Road. Smaller medical office buildings are often a faster install than a hospital campus because access and approvals are simpler.

What does a vending machine cost our property?

Nothing. Sidecar owns the equipment and covers installation, product, restocking, cash and card handling, and repairs. The property provides floor space of roughly the footprint of a filing cabinet and a standard electrical outlet. There is no purchase price, no lease and no monthly fee.
