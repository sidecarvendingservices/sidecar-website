Common Questions

Do you cover the high-rise office towers in Buckhead?

Yes, and they're the core of what we do here. Buckhead has over 50 high-rise buildings and more than 20.7 million square feet of office space. In buildings that tall, we plan multiple placements across floors, or a micro-market, under a single property-management agreement, and we work to each building's requirements for insurance, badging, dock scheduling and freight elevators.

How long does it take you to reach Buckhead?

About 14 minutes off-peak. Lenox and Peachtree Road are 8.4 road miles from our Sandy Springs base via GA-400 South, and GA-400 slows considerably at rush hour, so our drivers also run Roswell Road and Peachtree Road as alternates. Either way, service calls are handled same-day.

Which Buckhead zip codes do you cover?

30305, 30324, 30326, 30327 and 30342. Zip 30326 is the Lenox and Phipps office core and 30305 is west and central Buckhead — both ours alone. Zip 30324 (Lindbergh/Morosgo) is shared with our North Atlanta coverage, and 30327 and 30342 (Chastain Park, Mount Paran, North Buckhead) are shared with our Sandy Springs coverage.

Do you place machines in apartment or condominium towers?

Yes — it's one of the best-fitting verticals in Buckhead. Residential towers here reach 660 feet and concentrate around Peachtree/Piedmont, Buckhead Village and Lindbergh/Morosgo. A single tower can hold hundreds of units behind one amenity floor and one loading dock, which is efficient for us and convenient for your residents.

Is there enough demand with so many restaurants nearby?

Yes, and we'd rather be honest about why. Buckhead has more than 1,500 retail units, so lunch isn't the gap. The gap is the worker on the 34th floor who isn't spending 20 minutes on elevators and a walk, plus the early morning, mid-afternoon and evening hours restaurants don't cover. We compete on convenience and vertical distance, not on replacing a restaurant.

Do you serve dealerships or warehouses in Buckhead?

Yes — we'll place and service a machine at any business type in our coverage area, dealerships and warehouses included. Buckhead's own zip codes are built around office towers, retail and residential high-rises rather than an industrial district, so that's where the strongest concentration of demand sits here. If you run a dealership or warehouse, we also cover the Peachtree Boulevard corridor in neighboring Chamblee, where that kind of space is more common.
