Common Questions

Acworth vending, answered.

Do you actually cover Acworth, or just the closer parts of Cobb County?

We cover both Acworth zip codes, 30101 and 30102. Downtown Acworth is 26.8 road miles from our Sandy Springs base, about 38 minutes off-peak on I-75 North. We route Acworth on the same day as Marietta, Kennesaw and Woodstock, and we set restock intervals and machine capacity to match that route rather than promising a schedule the distance won't support.

Where are the business parks you serve in Acworth?

The main concentrations are the New McEver industrial area inside the city, and in 30102 the Northpoint-75 Business Park on Northpoint Parkway next to the Acworth Park & Ride, plus Cherokee 75 Business Park on Cherokee Parkway near Highway 92 and I-75 Exit 277. Cherokee 75 also markets a build-to-suit option up to roughly 70,000 square feet.

Our building has several small tenants. Does a vending machine make sense?

Often yes, but in a shared common area rather than inside one suite. Acworth's business-park inventory is shallow-bay space leased in smaller units, so a single tenant rarely has the headcount to support a dedicated machine, while a lobby or shared break area can serve the whole building. That conversation is usually with the landlord or property manager.

Is there a hospital or medical campus in Acworth you serve?

We'll serve any medical or dental practice in Acworth the same as any other business here — there's just no hospital or larger medical campus inside the city to point to yet. The nearest hospital-scale facilities are roughly nine to ten miles away in Marietta and Woodstock, both of which we also cover.

How often will you restock?

That depends on volume, and we'd rather set a realistic interval than an optimistic one. Because Acworth sits at the far end of our I-75 North route, we specify larger-capacity machines and a planogram that holds up between visits. If a site's volume justifies more frequent service, we increase it — but we'll tell you what to expect before installation, not after.
