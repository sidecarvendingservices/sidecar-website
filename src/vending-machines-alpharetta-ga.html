Yes. Windward is 4,100,000 square feet, the largest office complex in metro Atlanta, and Sanctuary Park is nine office buildings totalling 1,420,520 square feet. Both are campus-scale sites, so we'd plan multiple machines across buildings rather than one unit, and you can work through a single property manager for the whole park.

How fast can you get to Alpharetta from your base?

About 24 minutes off-peak. We operate from zip 30328 in Sandy Springs, 16.1 road miles from Windward Parkway up GA-400. That's an off-peak figure — GA-400 northbound at 5pm is slower, which is why we schedule Alpharetta service mid-morning or early afternoon and route it together with Milton and Roswell.

Which Alpharetta zip codes do you cover?

30004, 30005, 30009 and 30022. Zip 30004 is shared with Milton, and 30022's census centroid is actually in Johns Creek, so we cover businesses that describe themselves as being in any of those three cities. Coverage is the same regardless of which name is on your mail.

Is office vending worth it here given how much vacancy there is?

In most cases, yes. North Fulton submarket office vacancy was 25.5% at the end of 2025, so a building's square footage overstates its headcount on its own. But the submarket added 108,761 square feet of net absorption in the quarter, and Alpharetta's daytime population of 122,395 against roughly 66,500 residents means workers are genuinely coming in. We size machines to your occupied floors, not to the building's total footprint.

Would you stock a Class A office park the same way as an apartment community?

No, and that's on purpose. Alpharetta's office population is highly degree-holding with a high median household income, so office parks get cold brew, sparkling and functional beverages, protein and better-for-you snacks, and card and mobile payment as standard. Apartment amenity rooms see evening and weekend traffic instead, so those machines lean toward beverages and convenience essentials.

Does it cost anything to install a machine at our property?

No. Installation, delivery, restocking and repairs are free to you. We own and maintain the equipment and handle the product buying and the service calls. If a machine underperforms at your location, we relocate or remove it at no cost to you.
