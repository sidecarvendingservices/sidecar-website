Can you schedule restocking around our shift changes?

Yes, and in multi-shift buildings we prefer to. Restocking mid-shift means working around people; restocking at changeover means the machine is full when the incoming shift arrives. Tell us your shift pattern during the site walk and we'll build the service schedule around it.

Do you cover both Kennesaw zip codes?

Yes. 30144 covers Town Center, Chastain Meadows, Barrett Lakes and the eastern side of the city; 30152 covers West Kennesaw including the Stilesboro and Pine Mountain areas. Both are entirely within our service area, and neither is shared with another coverage area.

What does it cost the property to have a machine installed?

Nothing. Sidecar owns the equipment and covers installation, product, restocking, cash and card handling, and repairs. You provide floor space roughly the footprint of a filing cabinet and a standard electrical outlet — no purchase price, no lease and no monthly fee.

Do you place machines in apartment communities near Kennesaw State?

Yes. Kennesaw's attached and multi-family housing concentrates around Ridenour, the Barrett Lakes and Town Center corridor, and the intown and campus-adjacent areas. Student-adjacent buildings have a later, more caffeine-driven demand pattern than an office, so we stock them differently and make sure mobile payment is enabled on every machine.
